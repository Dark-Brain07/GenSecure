03. PROJECT OVERVIEW (Character Count: 962 / 1000 max)
================================================================================
Deterministic blockchains cannot read HTTPS endpoints or parse natural-language legal terms. Organizations publish critical warranties, privacy promises, and service level commitments, but users must blindly trust they will not silently change.

GenSecure is a decentralized assurance protocol natively powered by GenLayer. Protocol teams and organizations lock economic stake behind exact commitments tied to immutable canonical HTTPS URLs.

When activated, GenLayer validators independently fetch the live web terms and certify a semantic baseline. If community members suspect a breach, validators re-crawl the exact frozen endpoint via GenVM web rendering and evaluate semantic drift through comparative equivalence consensus (gl.eq_principle.prompt_comparative). A decoupled judge contract verifies whether deviations violate the immutable rules, automatically paying the challenger and distributing the stake—with zero centralized oracles or operator keys.
